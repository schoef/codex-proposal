1. Strongest and worth protecting
- The stronger framing is mostly earned: the draft now defines the project as a test of whether ttbar and ggH share a nontrivial gluon-PDF response direction, which is a real idea rather than generic "joint fit" inflation.
2. Weak / overreaching
- "Shared PDF directions" is still qualitative; main risk: after acceptance cuts and nuisance profiling the channels may constrain different linear combinations; acceptable only as a hypothesis; de-risk with explicit Fisher/response-overlap plots in (x1,x2,Q).
3. Claims needing evidence or tighter wording
- The overlap band (`x ~ 0.01-0.08`, `Q ~ 125 GeV to O(500 GeV)`) reads plausible but asserted; either derive it from the actual kernels/Asimov study or soften the wording to "candidate shared region."
- The "latest results/source anchors" section is not yet precise enough for a theoretical feasibility writeup: cite exact papers/notes, dates, and datasets, and avoid "latest" unless you say "as of 24 April 2026" what is latest.
- In particular, the claim that mature differential ttbar ingredients remain mostly Run-2/13 TeV while Run-3 top is still limited is time-sensitive and needs either a hard source or more cautious phrasing.
4. What the proposer should do next
- Add one compact table listing each branch/claim, its main risk, whether that risk is acceptable, and the concrete evidence or redesign that would de-risk it; if only one shared eigen-direction survives, that is still a valid feasibility result.
