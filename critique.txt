1. Strongest: The `ttbar + ggH` core is genuinely interesting because both processes interrogate the same gluon PDF in overlapping kinematics; protecting that shared-latent logic is the right center.
2. Weakest link: “Minimal process set” is not yet fully justified, because the Higgs side already expands into many observables and optional channels; explain which one or two unbinned Higgs observables carry unique PDF information beyond a standard mass fit, or cut the rest.
3. Unbinned choice: The top observables look defensible as a controlled basis, but the Higgs list (`pT^H`, `|y_H|`, `N_jets`, leading-jet `pT`, `Delta phi`) reads partly decorative; you need an information argument showing why unbinned treatment beats a sharp low-dimensional binned baseline here.
4. Evidence needed: Show that detector realism and background modeling do not erase the claimed Higgs-side gain; otherwise the proposal risks being “top inference plus a conventional Higgs appendage.”
5. Staging: Stages 1 to 3 are coherent, but Stage 4 changes the question from in-situ PDF calibration to PDF-SMEFT disentangling; that is a new proposal branch, not an obvious next step.
6. SMEFT risk: Main risk is severe degeneracy with the gluon PDF and rapid scope inflation; that risk is not acceptable now unless you first demonstrate nontrivial joint-fit information from `ttbar + ggH` in the SM-only setup.
7. SMEFT de-risking: Defer the SMEFT branch to a short “future extension” unless you can name a minimal operator set and a concrete closure test proving that unbinned kinematics separate PDF and SMEFT effects better than a binned analysis.
8. Fallbacks: The top-only fallback is credible; the Higgs and SMEFT fallbacks are weaker because they still assume the same machinery works without proving where the information survives.
9. Next move: Tighten the proposal around one minimal Higgs observable set, define the binned comparator explicitly, and state a decision rule for whether SMEFT is deferred until after Stage 3 succeeds.
