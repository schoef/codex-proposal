1. Strongest: The writeup’s best move is narrowing the shared layer to PDF coefficients and stating explicitly that overlap must be in response-weighted PDF sensitivity, not in naive kinematic ranges; that is real structure, not rhetoric.
2. Main risk: Stage 2 is not yet clearly identifiable, because once events are modeled as a signal-background mixture in `(m_gg,k)`, any background misspecification in `f_b(k)` or category migration can imitate signal-shape changes and push on the shared PDF coefficients through the mixture weights.
3. Why it matters: “PDFs only enter the signal block” is not sufficient protection; if the classifier/summary variables or categories are themselves sculpted by photon resolution, jet activity, or migration effects, sideband-constrained background can still leak into the inferred signal PDF dependence unless those distortions are separately parameterized and tested.
4. Weakest link: the proposed factorization `f_b(m_gg,k)=f_b^m(m_gg|k)f_b^k(k)` / conditional low-dimensional flow is plausible as an engineering starting point, but not yet realistic enough to defend as a physics assumption for unbinned inference without an explicit closure program.
5. Risk calibration: acceptable only if Stage 2 is reframed as a validation problem first; otherwise the fit can look elegant while learning residual background/category systematics rather than gluon-PDF information.
6. Evidence needed: show on Asimov and stress-test toys that sideband-fitted background families with induced `m_gg-k` correlations, category migration nuisances, and signal contamination do not generate fake pulls on the shared PDF directions when the truth has no Higgs-PDF shift.
7. Better move: make the first Higgs likelihood deliberately more conservative, e.g. unbinned in `m_gg` but binned or templated in a very small set of `k` observables/categories, with nuisance modes for migration and background-shape transfer from sidebands into signal region.
8. What to do next: define a concrete non-leakage criterion for Stage 2 (maximum spurious shift on each shared PDF eigen-direction under background/migration misspecification), and do not claim the fully unbinned factorization is defendable until that closure threshold is passed.
