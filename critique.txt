The quoted shared band `x ~ 0.01-0.08` and phrases like "clean shared region" read harder than the evidence; overlap in `(m_T,y)` is not yet overlap in Fisher information after acceptance, cuts, and backgrounds. Risk: medium; acceptable only if Stage 0 becomes a response-weight map, not a kinematic sketch.
3. The correlated-vs-specific uncertainty split is directionally sensible but still asserted, not shown. In particular, "possibly" correlated perturbative-shape modes and limited experimental correlations need either explicit examples or removal. Risk: high if left vague, because fake correlation is exactly how joint fits become scientifically empty; de-risk by listing only nuisances with a defensible shared parameterization.
4. The staged joint-fit case is not yet obviously stronger than a one-way `ttbar -> ggH` transport study. Stage 1 already admits the likely failure mode: the channels may constrain different PDF directions, in which case transport/consistency is the honest main result. Risk: high; acceptable if the writeup states joint fitting as a hypothesis to test, not the default payoff.
5. Claims needing tighter wording: "ggH pins the lower-x shoulder", "ttbar constrains the same gluon field after DGLAP evolution", and "the real consistency test" all need evidence about which PDF eigen-directions are shared rather than qualitative overlap language.
