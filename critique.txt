Strongest
- The writeup gets the main point right: the real shared handle is the gluon-PDF subspace, not a broad cancellation of detector nuisances, and that restraint is worth protecting.
Weak / overreach
- "Shared experimental uncertainties" is too loose: luminosity and beam energy can be correlated, but pileup, object calibrations, JES, and photon scales are not one defensible common block for a first ttbar+H->gammagamma fit.
- The ggH side is slightly under-ranked: in an unbinned diphoton fit the continuum-background parameterization and category migration are structural nuisances, not just small residual experimental systematics.
Claims needing evidence or tighter wording
- The latest CMS 13.6 TeV H->gammagamma fiducial result is 74 +- 11 (stat) +5/-4 (syst) fb, so "statistics-dominated" is defensible inclusively, but not automatically in differential or high-pT_H regions.
- alpha_s is not a clean standalone shared 1-3% nuisance unless varied consistently in PDF evolution and both hard processes; otherwise the writeup risks double counting or mis-correlating it.
- ttbar modeling should be split: perturbative scale/PDF pieces can be discussed alongside ggH, but shower, color-reconnection, hadronization, and top-mass effects are separate and probably more limiting than the current ranking admits.
What to do next
- Recast the map into three classes: truly shared (PDF, maybe alpha_s, lumi/beam), theory-homologous but not obviously correlated, and fully separate detector/background nuisances; cite one concrete source per size estimate.
- For a first fit, keep ggH to y_H and modest pT_H sensitivity; if hard-recoil bins are central, finite-m_t/resummation uncertainties may outrank the advertised PDF gain.
