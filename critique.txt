1. Strengths worth protecting
- The fixed top-defined basis, sign alignment, explicit projector/residual formula, and mandatory nuisance/mismatch scans make Stage 2 far more disciplined and close to reproducible.
2. Weakness / weakest link
- Stage 2 is not yet fully reproducible across groups because key analyst choices remain under-specified: the exact window weight, Fisher normalization, pseudoinverse regularization/rank cutoff, and numerical treatment of nearly-degenerate modes can move the residual verdict.
3. Claims needing tighter support
- The Higgs observable trio is plausible, but `pT_H` still needs a stronger argument that its incremental information is genuinely gluon-latent overlap rather than recoil/modelling leverage; show this with an ablation (`|y_H|` only, then add `pT_H`) under full profiling.
4. Risk assessment of the boldest branch
- Main risk: a surviving residual mode is a nuisance-model artifact; acceptable if the paper is framed as a transfer-vs-new-direction decision test, but de-risk it by requiring stability to alternative regularization choices and at least one independent implementation check.
5. What to do next
- Add a one-paragraph numerical convention block for Stage 2 and one ablation figure/table justifying `pT_H`; without those, the overlap test is sharper than before but still not convincingly portable, and the Higgs observable choice still reads partly convenience-driven.
