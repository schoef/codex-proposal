1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level in-situ fit of `ttbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared gluon latent; the convention is fixed once and for all as the top-only Fisher-orthonormal basis of `delta ln g(x,Q0)` at `Q0=m_t`, with sign chosen so each mode has positive overlap with the window-average on `x in [8e-3,8e-2]`, and this exact basis is DGLAP-evolved and reused unchanged in every later stage.
2. Frozen benchmark and observables: one Asimov truth model, one nominal nuisance model, unbinned `ttbar` in `(m_tt,|y_tt|)`, unbinned Higgs in `(m_gammagamma,|y_H|,pT_H)`, with `m_gammagamma` only for signal-background separation while the PDF information is quantified from `(|y_H|,pT_H)`.
3. Stage 1 closure: keep the smallest `k` with `>=95%` of the top-only PDF Fisher trace in the target window, require coefficient recovery within `0.3 sigma`, 68% coverage in `[60%,76%]`, and `>=10%` gain over a matched binned baseline on the target metric `M_g = Var[ integral_W dx w(x) delta ln g(x,Q=m_H) ]` with `w(x)=1/Delta ln x` on the same window.
4. Stage 2 reproducible overlap test: compute Higgs-only Fisher `F_H`, project it into the shared top basis, and define the residual `R_H = F_H - F_H P_t (P_t^T F_H P_t)^+ P_t^T F_H`, where `P_t` spans the retained top modes; summarize overlap by the residual eigenvalues and by the post-fit variance reduction on `M_g` when Higgs is added.
5. Operational decision rule: Higgs is only “transfer validation” if `lambda_max(R_H)<0.05*lambda_1(F_t)` or if adding Higgs improves `M_g` by `<10%`; Higgs becomes a genuine new direction only if one residual mode survives full `(PDF+nuisance)` profiling with `lambda_max(R_H)>=0.05*lambda_1(F_t)` and the joint fit reduces `Var(M_g)` by `>=15%` beyond top-only.
6. Mandatory nuisance decomposition: run `(i)` nuisances fixed/PDF floating, `(ii)` PDF fixed/nuisances floating, `(iii)` both floating; only residual modes already present in `(i)` and still present in `(iii)` count as proton-structure information, while modes appearing only through `(ii)` are classified as nuisance-induced pseudo-directions.
7. Alternate robustness check: repeat Stage 2 with a truth-injection mismatch in Higgs only, e.g. generate pseudo-data with a distorted nonperturbative/recoil `pT_H` tail or background-shape nuisance outside the fit model, and require the residual-mode claim to persist; if it disappears, downgrade the effect to modelling sensitivity rather than new PDF information.
8. Extension gate and risk: only if the nominal and mismatch-injected tests both pass do we open the SMEFT branch `{O_tG, delta y_t, c_HG}`; main risk is that Higgs adds no orthogonal gluon information after profiling, but that still yields a clean publishable statement that Higgs validates transfer of the top-calibrated gluon latent rather than discovering a new gluon direction.
