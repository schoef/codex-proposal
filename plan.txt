1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level unbinned fit of `ttbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared gluon latent, defined once from the top-only Fisher-orthonormal basis of `delta ln g(x,Q0)` at `Q0=m_t` and then DGLAP-evolved unchanged.
2. Minimal observables: `ttbar` provides the anchor; Higgs uses only unbinned `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|, pT_H)`, where `m_gammagamma` is the signal/background discriminator, `|y_H|` is the leading proxy for incoming-`x` asymmetry, and `pT_H` is treated as a hypothesis test for extra overlap information rather than assumed useful.
3. Reproducible setup: publish the exact Asimov/pseudo-data configuration `(luminosity, yields, retained mode count k, nuisance priors, detector response, seeds)` and a reference script that recomputes `F_t`, `F_H`, the top-span projector `P_t`, and residual Higgs information `R_H`.
4. Stage 1 closure: choose the smallest `k` retaining `>=95%` of the top-only Fisher trace on `x in [8e-3, 8e-2]`; require coefficient recovery within `0.3 sigma`, 68% coverage in `[60%,76%]`, and `>=10%` gain over a matched binned baseline on a Higgs-relevant gluon moment.
5. Stage 2 numerical conventions block: fix sign alignment to the window-averaged response on `x in [8e-3,8e-2]`, report all eigenvalues normalized to `lambda_1(F_t)`, define overlap with the Moore-Penrose projector in the retained top span, quote localization at `Q=m_H`, and use the same profiling order and prior conventions in every ablation.
6. Explicit Higgs ablation A (`|y_H|` only): run full `(PDF+nuisance)` profiling with Higgs observables `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|)` only; if `lambda_max(R_H)<0.05*lambda_1(F_t)` or the gain in the target gluon moment is `<10%`, classify Higgs as transfer validation rather than a new proton-structure direction.
7. Explicit Higgs ablation B (`|y_H| + pT_H`): add `pT_H` with all nuisances and priors unchanged; claim genuine new Higgs overlap information only if the residual survives full profiling, improves the target moment by `>=15%` beyond ablation A, and remains localized with `>=80%` of squared response norm in `x in [6e-3,3e-2]`.
8. Nuisance stress tests: repeat ablations with `(i)` nuisances fixed/PDF floating, `(ii)` PDF fixed/nuisances floating, `(iii)` both floating, and a harsher Higgs benchmark with doubled shape-prior widths plus recoil-tail/background-slope correlation `rho=0.8`; `pT_H` counts only if its residual is already visible in `(i)` and survives harsh `(iii)`.
9. Robustness to modelling mismatch: generate pseudo-data with an unfitted recoil-tail or background-shape distortion; if the `pT_H` residual disappears, delocalizes, or is absorbed by nuisance modes, reclassify it as modelling sensitivity and use that to define the control precision needed before claiming Higgs-top gluon synergy.
10. Extension gate and risk: only if the nominal, harsh-prior, and mismatch tests all pass do we open the SMEFT branch `{O_tG, delta y_t, c_HG}`; the main risk is that `pT_H` adds no orthogonal gluon information after profiling, but that is still publishable because it cleanly shows that top data calibrate the relevant gluon latent and Higgs mainly validates transfer.
