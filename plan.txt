1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level in-situ fit of `ttbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared low-dimensional gluon-PDF latent, and ask the sharp question: when does Higgs become a genuine PDF-constraining channel rather than only a transfer validation from top?
2. Frozen Asimov benchmark for all go/no-go decisions: `ttbar` lepton+jets with `N=2.0e5` selected events and `ggH->gamma gamma` with `N_sig=6.0e3`, `N_bkg=2.4e4`, at one fixed luminosity/selection/reco setup; this benchmark is declared once and reused unchanged for surrogate training targets, binned comparison, closure, and stress tests.
3. Observables and binning are frozen: unbinned `ttbar` uses `(m_tt, |y_tt|)` and unbinned Higgs uses `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|, pT_H)`; the matched binned reference uses `m_tt` edges `[350, 500, 700, 1000, 1600] GeV`, `|y_tt|` edges `[0, 0.4, 0.8, 1.2, 2.5]`, `|y_H|` edges `[0, 0.3, 0.6, 0.9, 1.2, 2.5]`, `pT_H` edges `[0, 20, 45, 80] GeV`, and `m_gammagamma` edges `[120, 122, 124, 126, 128, 130] GeV`.
4. Nuisance/background model is also frozen: luminosity, acceptance/efficiency, jet-energy scale/resolution, photon energy scale/resolution, `ttbar` scale/reweighting, Higgs recoil modeling, and one correlated PDF-basis prior; Higgs background is an exponential-times-polynomial continuum with free normalization plus slope/curvature constrained by sidebands, and `ttbar` background is one effective normalization-plus-shape nuisance covering non-`ttbar` contamination.
5. Stage 1 is top-only closure: on Asimov and toy variations, the unbinned fit must recover every injected gluon-latent coefficient within `0.3 sigma`, give nominal 68% interval coverage in `[60%, 76%]`, and beat or match the frozen binned baseline by `>=10%` in posterior width for the Higgs-relevant gluon combination; risk is surrogate/PDF-basis misspecification, fallback is reducing latent dimension before adding Higgs.
6. Stage 2 is Higgs-only increment and stress: adding `|y_H|` then `pT_H` must improve the propagated gluon uncertainty in the target `x` window (`10^-2` to `10^-1`) by `>=15%` relative to top-only propagation on the same benchmark, while fake improvements from background or recoil nuisances remain `<5%`; risk is that Higgs contributes mostly nuisance sensitivity, which is still informative because it defines the minimal useful Higgs observable set.
7. Exact Stage-3 block condition: Stage 3 is forbidden unless the frozen benchmark passes all closure/stress tests, namely no latent bias `>0.5 sigma`, no pull width outside `[0.8, 1.2]`, no coverage below `60%`, no Asimov likelihood-ratio miscalibration above `10%`, and no stress-test degradation larger than `25%` under alternative shower/recoil/background truth; any single failure means the proposal stops at calibrated top-to-Higgs transfer rather than claiming a joint in-situ fit.
8. Sparkling extension, only if Stage 3 unlocks: promote the fitted gluon latent to a portable prior for a minimal SMEFT branch `{O_tG, delta y_t, c_HG}` and test whether the same detector-level events separate proton-structure distortions from hard-process deformations; risk is strong degeneracy, fallback is to publish the irreducible degeneracy map and the transferable gluon prior as the main result.
