1. Core thesis: test whether detector-level unbinned `ttbar` and fiducial `ggH(H->gammagamma)` data admit a scientifically defensible joint fit with one latent gluon-PDF basis plus a conservative correlated-nuisance model, rather than assuming transferability from `ttbar` to Higgs.
2. Stage-1 gate after fiducial selection: build matched event-level sensitivity atlases in the accepted `y`, `pT/mT`, and inferred `(x1,x2,Q)` support, then compute (i) a shared-score metric `S = Tr(F_cross)/sqrt[Tr(F_tt)Tr(F_H)]` from per-event PDF-score Fisher blocks and (ii) a nuisance-correlation metric `C = sum_k w_k cos(theta_k)` over theory/experimental nuisance response vectors restricted to the common phase space.
3. Go/no-go rule for a true joint fit: proceed only if `S >= 0.3` for at least two leading gluon-PDF directions and `C >= 0.5` for the dominant nominally shared nuisances (`PDF`, `alpha_s`, luminosity if common dataset, beam energy, ISR/recoil modeling); otherwise declare that overlap insufficient for cancellation and do not claim a full joint likelihood.
4. Why this gate belongs first: it converts the vague “overlap question” into a falsifiable precondition, prevents over-engineering a coupled SBI system when the channels only weakly constrain the same latent directions, and yields a publishable no-go map even if the joint fit fails.
5. Stage-2 joint-fit path if the gate passes: fit `ttbar`, `ggH`, and combined data with the same latent PDF basis, allow only empirically supported nuisance couplings, and use cross-channel score-consistency residuals as a diagnostic for missing higher orders or mis-modeled correlations before interpreting tension as PDF information.
6. Stage-3 perturbative ingredients: `ttbar` needs NNLO-informed differential reweighting on NLO+PS events with explicit `m_t`-scheme control; `ggH` needs high-order inclusive normalization plus fiducial `y_H/pT_H` modeling with low-`pT` resummation and finite-top-mass control in the accepted region.
7. Sequential fallback if the gate fails: perform `ttbar -> latent gluon PDF -> ggH` propagation only, treat Higgs as an external validation channel, and quantify how much of the remaining discrepancy is explained by missing shared-score support versus absent nuisance correlation.
8. Validation path in both branches: closure tests on pseudo-data with shifted PDFs, `alpha_s`, scales, shower/recoil, and detector systematics; require agreement with a matched binned baseline and explicit stress tests where one channel is intentionally mis-modeled.
9. Main risks: fiducial overlap may collapse after realistic `H->gammagamma` cuts, nuisance “correlation” may be acceptance-driven rather than theory-driven, and surrogate bias may mimic gain; all are acceptable because each yields a concrete failure mode and a sharper statement of what theory or measurement improvements are needed.
10. Scientific payoff: either a genuine joint unbinned `ttbar+ggH` gluon-PDF determination with defensible shared information and partial uncertainty cancellation, or a rigorous sequential/no-go result that identifies the exact missing overlap and correlation structures blocking such a fit today.
