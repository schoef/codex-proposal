1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level in-situ fit of `t tbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared low-dimensional gluon-PDF latent, asking the narrow question: does Higgs add PDF information beyond top, rather than merely validate top-to-Higgs transfer?
2. Minimal Higgs observable set: keep the mass fit conventional and treat only `( |y_H| , pT_H )` as the Higgs PDF-sensitive payload; start with `|y_H|` alone as the guaranteed low-risk handle on incoming-parton `x`, add coarse `pT_H` only in a low-recoil region where modeling is controlled, and exclude jet multiplicity / leading-jet observables from the core because they are mainly QCD-model/systematics carriers rather than clean new PDF information.
3. Explicit low-dimensional comparator: benchmark the unbinned SBI against a matched binned likelihood using `t tbar: (m_tt, |y_tt|)` and `ggH: (|y_H|, pT_H)` with a fixed small binning such as `4 x 3` for top and `4 x 2` for Higgs, chosen once from expected Fisher information and then frozen; this is the mandatory reference, not an afterthought.
4. Stage 1: top-only closure and calibration. Risk: the surrogate or PDF basis is misspecified. Why acceptable: this isolates the machinery. Fallback: reduce the PDF latent dimension and restrict to the most robust `t tbar` category while preserving the same binned comparator.
5. Stage 2: Higgs-only increment test. Quantify the marginal PDF information in `|y_H|` and then in `(|y_H|, pT_H)` after detector realism and sideband-constrained backgrounds. Risk: Higgs adds negligible information. Why acceptable: that null result directly defines the minimal useful Higgs observable set.
6. Stage 3: joint fit decision rule. Promote Higgs from validation to a true fit ingredient only if, at fixed nuisance model, it improves the gluon-PDF precision or KL/Fisher information in the Higgs-relevant `x` range by a predeclared threshold (for example `>20%` relative to top-only propagation) and passes closure under theory/detector variations.
7. SMEFT is conditional, not baseline: open a Stage-3b SMEFT branch only if Stage 3 succeeds by that rule; then test the minimal coefficient set `{O_tG, delta y_t, c_HG}` to ask whether the unbinned joint fit separates PDF distortions from genuine hard-process deformations.
8. SMEFT risk/fallback: the main risk is PDF-SMEFT degeneracy overwhelming the Higgs gain. Why acceptable: even failure maps the irreducible degeneracy surface. Fallback: report Stage-3 PDF posteriors as priors and quantify only the residual decorrelation power, without claiming a full simultaneous SMEFT fit.
