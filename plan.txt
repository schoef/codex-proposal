1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level in-situ fit of `ttbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared low-dimensional gluon-PDF latent, and ask the sharp question: does Higgs add an independent gluon-response direction beyond what `ttbar` already calibrates?
2. Frozen benchmark and observables: reuse one fixed Asimov setup for all decisions, with unbinned `ttbar` in `(m_tt, |y_tt|)` and unbinned Higgs in `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|, pT_H)`, plus one matched binned baseline and one frozen nuisance model (luminosity, reco/scale, recoil, background, theory-shape).
3. Stage 1, top-only closure: recover injected gluon-latent coefficients within `0.3 sigma`, achieve 68% coverage in `[60%,76%]`, and beat or match the binned baseline by `>=10%` on the Higgs-relevant gluon combination; risk is surrogate/PDF-basis misspecification, fallback is reducing latent dimension before adding Higgs.
4. Stage 2, explicit overlap test: compute the leading gluon-response eigenmodes for `ttbar` alone, Higgs alone, and the joint fit in the same latent basis, then quantify whether Higgs adds a new direction by the principal-angle / residual-Fisher spectrum after projecting Higgs modes onto the `ttbar` span.
5. Stage 2a, three comparison toggles are mandatory: repeat the overlap test with `(i)` nuisances fixed and PDF floating, `(ii)` PDF fixed and nuisances floating, and `(iii)` both floating; only a mode that survives as new in case `(iii)` counts as genuine Higgs PDF information.
6. Stage 2b, claim rule: quote Higgs PDF gain only if the surviving joint-only mode reduces the posterior variance in the target gluon combination or `x` window (`10^-2` to `10^-1`) by `>=10-15%`; if the gain disappears once nuisances or the shared PDF latent float, the result is reclassified as transfer validation or nuisance calibration, not new PDF constraint.
7. Stage 3 gate and extension: only after passing closure plus the overlap test do we promote the fitted gluon prior into a minimal SMEFT branch `{O_tG, delta y_t, c_HG}` to test separation of proton-structure and hard-process distortions; risk is degeneracy, fallback is to publish the degeneracy map and portable gluon prior as the main result.
8. Global risk logic: the main risk is that Higgs sensitivity is nearly collinear with `ttbar` once detector and theory nuisances are profiled, but this is still a clean scientific outcome because it sharply defines where joint fits help, where they only stabilize extrapolation, and which Higgs observables are worth keeping.
