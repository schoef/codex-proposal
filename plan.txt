1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level unbinned fit of `ttbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared gluon latent, defined once from the top-only Fisher-orthonormal basis of `delta ln g(x,Q0)` at `Q0=m_t` and then DGLAP-evolved unchanged.
2. Pre-registered ggH functional: fix one Higgs-motivated gluon functional before any ablation, `Phi_ggH = int dx w_ggH(x) delta ln g(x,Q=m_H)`, with `w_ggH(x)` the normalized LO `gg->H` parton-luminosity weight after the fiducial `ggH->gamma gamma` acceptance map; this is the only scalar proton-structure target reported thereafter.
3. Sole gain metric: define `Gain(Phi_ggH) = Var_binned(Phi_ggH | full profiling) / Var_method(Phi_ggH | full profiling)` against a matched binned baseline; every stage, Higgs ablation, and robustness test is judged only by this ratio plus coverage/bias on `Phi_ggH`, not by any second moment or alternate score.
4. Minimal observables: `ttbar` provides the anchor; Higgs uses only unbinned `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|, pT_H)`, where `m_gammagamma` separates signal/background, `|y_H|` probes incoming-`x`, and `pT_H` is treated strictly as a possible extra handle on `Phi_ggH`.
5. Reproducible setup: publish the exact Asimov/pseudo-data configuration `(luminosity, yields, retained mode count k, nuisance priors, detector response, seeds)` and a reference script that recomputes `F_t`, `F_H`, the top-span projector `P_t`, and the profiled posterior variance of `Phi_ggH`.
6. Stage 1 closure: choose the smallest `k` retaining `>=95%` of the top-only Fisher trace on `x in [8e-3,8e-2]`; require coefficient recovery within `0.3 sigma`, 68% coverage in `[60%,76%]`, and `Gain(Phi_ggH) >= 1.10`.
7. Higgs ablation A (`|y_H|` only): run full `(PDF+nuisance)` profiling with Higgs observables `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|)` only; if `Gain(Phi_ggH) < 1.10`, classify Higgs as transfer validation rather than a new proton-structure direction.
8. Higgs ablation B (`|y_H| + pT_H`): add `pT_H` with all nuisances and priors unchanged; claim genuine Higgs-added information only if `Gain(Phi_ggH)` improves by `>=15%` relative to ablation A under the same profiling conventions.
9. Robustness tests: repeat the nominal fit with `(i)` nuisances fixed/PDF floating, `(ii)` PDF fixed/nuisances floating, `(iii)` both floating, plus doubled Higgs shape-prior widths and injected recoil-tail/background-shape mismodelling; if `Gain(Phi_ggH)` collapses, coverage fails, or bias on `Phi_ggH` exceeds `0.3 sigma`, reclassify the Higgs contribution as modelling-sensitive.
10. Risk and fallback: the main risk is that `pT_H` adds no stable information on `Phi_ggH` after profiling, but this is still publishable because it would show, in one pre-registered ggH-relevant metric, that unbinned `ttbar` already calibrates the gluon direction that controls gluon-fusion Higgs predictions.
