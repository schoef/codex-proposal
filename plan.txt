1. Core thesis: formulate a joint unbinned likelihood for detector-level `ttbar` and `ggH(H->gammagamma)` events in which a common latent gluon-PDF parameterization, correlated nuisance model, and process-specific EFT/QCD theory layers are fit simultaneously rather than propagated sequentially.
2. Theoretical situation to establish first: map the actual overlap in `(x1,x2,Q,y,pT)` support between Run-2 `ttbar` and `ggH` observables, separating the safe common region (`mT_H ~ m_tt/2`, moderate `|y|`, low-to-intermediate recoil) from edge regions where top-mass effects, threshold logs, or boosted tails make a naive shared-theory treatment invalid.
3. Stage A, overlap kinematics: build response-level “PDF sensitivity atlases” for both channels, quantify where the score vectors in gluon-PDF coefficient space are aligned vs complementary, and define a fit basis of common latent directions plus channel-private residual directions; risk: overlap may be narrower than expected, fallback: restrict the joint fit to the common window and use the rest as one-way validation.
4. Stage B, correlated uncertainties: decompose nuisances into genuinely shared (`PDF`, `alpha_s`, beam energy, luminosity if same dataset, common nonperturbative ISR modeling) versus only partially shared (ren./fac. scale response, parton shower acceptance, photon isolation/recoil modeling) versus unshared (`m_t`, top decay and b-fragmentation, Higgs mass-window/background model, photon efficiencies); risk: over-claiming cancellation, fallback: start from a conservative block-diagonal nuisance model and promote correlations only when theory variations induce demonstrably aligned shape responses.
5. Stage C, perturbative requirements for `ttbar`: require at minimum NNLO QCD inclusive normalization plus state-of-the-art differential predictions in the variables used for inference, explicit control of `m_t` scheme dependence, and fast reweighting grids for PDF/scale variations; risk: full detector-level multi-differential theory response is too slow, fallback: anchor the unbinned surrogate to NNLO-informed parton-level reweighting on top of NLO+PS event generation.
6. Stage D, perturbative requirements for `ggH`: treat inclusive normalization with the HXSWG/YR-style high-order result, but differential `y_H`/`pT_H`/jet-bin structure must include finite-top-mass effects, resummation at low `pT`, and a controlled description of Higgs recoil for the diphoton fiducial selection; risk: the high-`pT` region breaks the common approximation, fallback: exclude or separately parameterize boosted `ggH` tails and keep the joint fit focused on the moderate-`pT` region where PDF leverage is still substantial.
7. Stage E, fit architecture: compare three nested fits — `ttbar` only, `ggH` only, and joint — using the same latent PDF basis and unbinned SBI machinery; the sparkling idea is to use cross-channel score-consistency as a theory-diagnostics observable, i.e. disagreement between the top-inferred and Higgs-inferred score fields flags missing higher orders or mis-modeled correlations before it is interpreted as PDF information.
8. Validation program: perform closure on pseudo-data generated with shifted PDFs, altered `alpha_s`, and deliberately mismatched showers/scales; then test robustness against known present-day experimental reality, namely precise Run-2 `ttbar` differentials that no single prediction describes perfectly in all bins and `H->gammagamma` differential measurements that are statistics-limited but theory-clean in inclusive rate; risk: apparent gains come from surrogate bias, fallback: require agreement with a matched binned/profile-likelihood baseline and publish failure modes as a result about where unbinned joint fits cease to be trustworthy.
9. Scientific payoff and decision logic: if the shared latent directions dominate, the project demonstrates an internally cross-calibrated gluon-PDF determination relevant for `ggH`; if correlations are weak, it still yields a concrete no-go map identifying which uncertainties (`m_t`, recoil/resummation, shower acceptance, high-`pT` finite-mass effects) prevent a sensible joint fit and therefore what theory work would most increase future feasibility.
