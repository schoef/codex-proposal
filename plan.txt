1. Core thesis: perform a joint detector-level in-situ fit of `ttbar` and `ggH->gamma gamma` with one shared gluon-PDF latent defined once and reused everywhere: the latent basis is the top-only Fisher-orthonormal eigenbasis of `delta ln g(x,Q=m_t)` around the nominal PDF, ordered by descending `ttbar` information and transported unchanged into the Higgs likelihood.
2. Frozen benchmark and observables: keep one Asimov setup, one nuisance model, unbinned `ttbar` in `(m_tt, |y_tt|)`, and unbinned Higgs in `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|, pT_H)`; this Higgs set is PDF-sensitive because `|y_H|` localizes the incoming-parton `x1,x2`, `pT_H` migrates events between threshold and harder-radiation regimes with different effective gluon `x`, and `m_gammagamma` stabilizes signal extraction so the PDF leverage is not washed out by background modelling.
3. Stage 1, top-only closure: retain `k=6` gluon modes, chosen as the smallest set capturing `>=95%` of the top-only PDF Fisher trace in the target window, and require recovery of injected coefficients within `0.3 sigma`, 68% coverage in `[60%,76%]`, and `>=10%` improvement over a matched binned baseline on the Higgs-relevant gluon quantity.
4. Stage 2, operational overlap test: target the gluon shape in `x in [8e-3, 8e-2]` at `Q=m_H`, summarized both by the window-averaged `delta ln g` and by the residual Fisher spectrum after projecting Higgs-only information onto the span of the first `k=6` top modes in the shared basis.
5. Stage 2 decision rule: keep at most `r=2` candidate Higgs-only residual modes, and declare a genuinely new direction only if its projected residual Fisher eigenvalue exceeds `5%` of the leading top eigenvalue and survives the full `(PDF+nuisance)` profiling test; otherwise classify the effect as transfer validation or nuisance-stabilized extrapolation.
6. Stage 2 mandatory toggles: run the overlap test for `(i)` nuisances fixed/PDF floating, `(ii)` PDF fixed/nuisances floating, and `(iii)` both floating; only modes present in `(i)` and still visible in `(iii)` count as genuine Higgs PDF information, while modes appearing only in `(ii)` diagnose nuisance leverage rather than proton-structure gain.
7. Alternate benchmark variation before any SMEFT branch: repeat Stages 1-2 with the Higgs channel reduced to `(m_gammagamma, |y_H|)` only, i.e. remove `pT_H` and its recoil/theory-shape dependence, to test whether any claimed overlap-breaking mode is robust or is being driven by modelling of Higgs transverse momentum rather than shared gluon structure.
8. Gate to extensions and risk logic: only if the nominal and reduced-Higgs benchmarks both show a surviving residual mode with `>=10-15%` variance reduction on the target gluon quantity do we open the SMEFT branch `{O_tG, delta y_t, c_HG}`; main risk is near-collinearity of Higgs and top information after profiling, but even failure yields a publishable map of which Higgs observables genuinely constrain the same gluon latent and which merely inherit the top calibration.
