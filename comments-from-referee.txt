- Strongest aspects
- The outline is now more ambitious and more memorable. It no longer reads as only a clever PDF-method proposal; it now has a sharper physics arc: use real CMS `ttbar(2l)` data to constrain the gluon, validate that constraint in real CMS `ggH`, and then exploit the calibrated proton input in top-Higgs measurements where PDF freedom and BSM-sensitive effects can genuinely compete. That is closer to the “sparkling” standard the updated brief asks for.
- The anchor measurement remains strong and fundable. A real-data detector-level unbinned constrained-gluon extraction from CMS `ttbar(2l)` is still a serious standalone result and remains the right spine for the whole program.
- The `H -> gamma gamma` validation is still a particularly good choice. It is experimentally clean, already established inside CMS, and a credible place to demonstrate whether the `ttbar`-derived gluon information is physically useful in another gluon-dominated measurement.
- The top-Higgs extension is not obviously decorative. There is now a real conceptual reason for it to belong: recent simultaneous PDF+SMEFT fit work argues that proton-structure freedom and EFT effects can interfere with one another if not treated consistently, and the proposed polynomial SBI-PDF machinery is unusually well matched to attacking that problem in one framework. That makes the add-on scientifically differentiated rather than just fashionable.
- The single-top follow-on is also not crazy scope inflation if kept as written. Nearby literature supports that single-top observables carry PDF sensitivity, including dependence tied to flavor-scheme choices and the perturbative `g -> b bbar` origin of the bottom PDF. As a focused cross-check, it can reinforce the gluon story rather than dilute it.
- On realism grounds, the hierarchy of channels is sensible: CMS differential `H -> gamma gamma` measurements clearly exist as a practical validation target, CMS has real `ttH/tH` and top-Yukawa/CP analyses that make the top-Higgs stage believable as a stretch direction, and single-top is realistic only in the compact cross-check role suggested here.

- Critical weaknesses
- The outline is now stronger scientifically, but it has moved back toward the feasibility boundary. The `ttH/tH` stage is genuinely interesting, but it is also a serious measurement-and-interpretation program in its own right. This matters because a reviewer may now worry that the proposal is trying to do one excellent core measurement plus one Higgs validation plus one ambitious SMEFT interpretation within a single grant. Keep saying, explicitly and repeatedly, that publication 1 alone is already full success and that the top-Higgs stage is contingent on the gluon program landing cleanly.
- The top-Higgs scope is now much better calibrated. Making `ttH` the default main target and retaining `tH` only as a secondary interference-sensitive lever is the right move and should be preserved in the full proposal.
- The realism check also argues for caution in how the top-Higgs stage is sold. CMS has relevant `ttH/tH` measurements and coupling-structure analyses, but a full real-data differential joint gluon-plus-SMEFT interpretation is still a stretch objective, not an analysis that already looks turnkey. This matters because the proposal becomes more credible if it presents top-Higgs as the exciting third-stage payoff of the same machinery, not as something nearly as mature as the `ttbar` or `H -> gamma gamma` steps.
- The single-top cross-check is plausible, but the proposal should resist overselling it as a direct gluon measurement. In standard treatments, single-top is also very sensitive to quark PDFs, electroweak structure, and flavor-scheme choices. This matters because the cross-check only helps if it is framed honestly: as an orthogonal consistency test of the constrained-gluon picture and bottom-generation logic, not as a second clean gluon anchor.
- The proposer has now mostly fixed this language. Preserve the current compact framing of single-top as a surgical cross-check and explicitly not a second anchor or second direct gluon-extraction pillar.
- The proposal now risks sounding slightly too elegant in its story of “top calibrates Higgs calibrates top-Higgs.” The chain is attractive, but the text should keep acknowledging where the experimental and theoretical interfaces are hardest: Higgs differential statistics, `ttH/tH` systematics, and robustness of joint PDF-versus-SMEFT separation. That matters because greatness is easier to fund when the proposer looks fully aware of the hardest failure modes.

- Feasibility concerns
- The program is now closer to “bold but fundable” than to merely “safe,” which is good, but only if the hierarchy is policed hard. The CMS `ttbar(2l)` constrained-gluon result must remain the only guaranteed flagship result. The `H -> gamma gamma` validation should remain the first payoff. The `ttH/tH` stage should be written as the distinctive stretch physics objective, not as a co-equal guaranteed deliverable.
- It also helps that the outline now says this plainly: publication 1 alone is grant-level success. That sentence should survive into the full proposal because it materially improves credibility.
- It is likewise helpful that Stage 3 is now written explicitly as the stretch objective and that `ttH` is now clearly first while `tH` is secondary. Those scope controls should survive intact.
- It is also useful that the fallback logic now names the `ttH`-centered SMEFT extension explicitly. That makes the stretch nature of Stage 3 look intentional rather than accidental.
- The manpower case is still defensible for a `2.5 MEUR` program, but only because the measurements sit in one experimental ecosystem and one inference philosophy. If the full proposal re-expands the top-Higgs or single-top pieces into broad subprograms, that defense weakens quickly.
- Theory-systematics ownership is even more central now than before. Once the proposal claims it can help disentangle proton structure from BSM-sensitive top-Higgs effects, generator accuracy, shower modeling, flavor-scheme dependence, and the treatment of theory nuisances become scientifically constitutive, not supporting detail. This is a serious strength if owned clearly and a serious weakness if glossed over.

- Novelty concerns
- The novelty claim is now stronger because it is no longer just “unbinned beats binned.” The genuinely new part is the attempt to connect detector-level unbinned gluon determination to a later separation of proton-structure and SMEFT effects in top-Higgs data. That is distinctive and worth keeping.
- The remaining novelty danger is overclaiming the top-Higgs stage before the gluon-calibration stage exists. The proposal should present this as a uniquely enabled extension of the same machinery, not as if the joint gluon-plus-SMEFT result were already near at hand.
- Avoid any wording that sounds like this program replaces global PDF or global SMEFT analyses in general. The sharper and more defensible claim is that it opens a new CMS-internal route to constraining the specific proton-structure ambiguities most relevant to selected Higgs and top-Higgs measurements.

- Structural recommendations
- Keep the narrative chain exactly as now written: `ttbar(2l)` constrained-gluon -> `ggH, H -> gamma gamma` validation -> `ttH`-first joint gluon-plus-SMEFT interpretation. That is the proposal’s identity.
- Tighten the top-Higgs wording slightly so the reader sees one main target, not two or three. The cleanest phrasing is: default stretch target `ttH`; use `tH` only where it adds specific interference leverage or coupling discrimination.
- The proposer has now largely done this correctly. Preserve that exact calibration rather than reopening it later in drafting.
- Keep single-top in the proposal only as a focused orthogonal cross-check. The current “supporting physics handle” framing is close to right and should not be allowed to grow.
- Preserve the fallback hierarchy very visibly. The more sparkling the late-stage physics becomes, the more important it is that the proposal keeps reminding the reader that the grant already succeeds if the constrained-gluon `ttbar(2l)` measurement lands.

- What to cut
- Cut any wording that makes `ttH`, `tH`, and single-top sound like three independent expansion fronts. The proposal is strongest when it has one stretch front, not several.
- Cut any implication that the single-top stage is a second direct gluon-extraction pillar. That would weaken credibility.
- Cut generic statements about “unlocking BSM” unless they are tied directly to the specific PDF-versus-SMEFT ambiguity the proposal says it can reduce.

- What to emphasize
- Emphasize that the proposal has found a rarer and stronger balance: it is now both sharper and more exciting.
- Emphasize that the most compelling feature is not the use of ML itself, but the prospect of turning proton-structure control into an enabling ingredient for top-Higgs interpretation inside CMS.
- Emphasize that the extension channels are worth keeping precisely because they are no longer random add-ons: `ggH` validates the gluon calibration, `ttH` exploits it where BSM interpretation needs it, and single-top stress-tests one specific theoretical link.
- Emphasize that this is now best described as a bold but still fundable program, provided the hierarchy of guaranteed result versus stretch objective is kept explicit.

- Optional stretch ideas worth keeping only if tightly integrated
- `tH` is worth mentioning only as a secondary lever on interference structure and coupling sensitivity relative to `ttH`, not as an equal measurement frontier.
- A single-top check is worth keeping only in the compact cross-check role currently suggested. If it grows beyond that, it will start to dilute the story.

- Bottom-line judgment
- This version is more interesting than the previous one. It has more scientific personality and a more memorable endpoint, and under the updated referee instructions I would count that as a real gain, not a liability.
- The right referee advice is therefore not “cut back to safety,” but “protect the hierarchy.” Keep the `ttbar(2l)` gluon extraction as the guaranteed flagship, keep `H -> gamma gamma` as the concrete validation, and keep the top-Higgs SMEFT stage as the proposal’s distinctive stretch objective.
